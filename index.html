Planning model, not a forecast. The unit of work is the economy-questionnaire (BL: Property Transfer and Construction Permitting, separate expert pools), with tiering, retention, and reform incidence applied per questionnaire — so an economy can be light on one questionnaire and heavy on the other. Per-questionnaire cost = Dynamics/SuSo process overhead (reducible by tooling) + fresh Pillar II/III content (reducible only by instrument design) + tier add-on for the Pillar I / recruitment side; economy-level shared work (cross-questionnaire consistency and final coding) is added per economy. Carry-forward, where invoked, applies to Pillar I only; expert non-response produces Pillar II/III data gaps rather than cost savings. Both clocks must close: collection-side work within the Dec–May window, and all work within the span ending Jul 15. TL time is excluded throughout, consistent with the 2026 reference cost of ~1.25 AW per economy across both questionnaires. The holiday shutdown is converted at 5 working days per analyst-week and deducted from analyst capacity on both clocks; expert-side elapsed time is not reduced, since questionnaires remain in the field over the break.
